Confirm it's your macropad

Press these keys on the device, in the order shown. The LED flashes white while it is waiting; the presses are not sent to your computer.

Wi-Fi setup

The macropad is in setup mode. Pick your network; it reboots and joins it, then the UI is at http://macropad.local or its IP address.

Profiles

On the device: long-press the encoder button to choose a profile. Turn the encoder to browse (the LED blinks the profile's colour), then short-press to activate it, or press key 1–9 to jump straight to that profile. Another long press cancels. A short press outside this mode sends the encoder button's own action.

Device

With Wi-Fi disabled the radio is free for Bluetooth. The setup access point starts only when no network is saved, or when key 1 is held while plugging in.

Wi-Fi networks

Up to 5 networks. The strongest one in range is used and the others are fallbacks; networks not seen in a scan (hidden ones) are tried last. Stored passwords are never shown – leave the field empty to keep one.
